Floatdoro is a focused Pomodoro timer for Mac that stays visible while you work.

Keep a clean countdown above your other windows, or control everything from the menu bar. Floatdoro is designed to remain useful without becoming another app you need to manage.

STAY IN THE FLOW

• Keep the floating timer above every workspace
• Resize it from a compact reminder to a task-focused panel
• Start, pause, reset, or skip an interval in one click
• See the current task without changing windows

PLAN THE NEXT STEP

• Add a lightweight queue of work tasks
• Complete the current task and move automatically to the next
• Review completed tasks and focus sessions in weekly history

MAKE IT YOURS

• Use 25/5, 50/10, and 90/20 presets
• Set custom focus and break durations
• Rename work and rest modes
• Choose a light or dark appearance
• Optionally start breaks automatically when focus ends
• Enable completion sounds and launch at login

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Floatdoro has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Your timer state, tasks, preferences, and focus history remain on your Mac.
