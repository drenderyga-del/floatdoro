Floatdoro now has a calmer, clearer interface built for quick glances:

• Redesigned the menu bar panel, floating timer, Settings, and History.
• Added a timer icon beside the live menu bar countdown.
• Starting an interval no longer opens an extra window; the floating timer stays under your control.
• Made the automatic break option easier to find in Settings.
• Improved long task titles, queue counts, keyboard navigation, and Reduce Motion behavior.

Your timer data, tasks, settings, and history remain stored locally on your Mac.
