Floatdoro support

Help with the floating timer, menu bar controls, notifications, and local data.

How do I open Floatdoro?

Click the Floatdoro countdown in the macOS menu bar. Floatdoro is a menu bar utility, so it does not show a Dock icon.

How do I close the app?

Use the power button in the main panel, or right-click the Floatdoro timer in the menu bar and choose “Quit Floatdoro.”

Where is my data?

Tasks, timer state, preferences, and history stay locally inside the app’s sandbox on your Mac.

Report an issue

Open a public request in Floatdoro Issues on GitHub. Include your macOS version, the Floatdoro version, and the steps needed to reproduce the problem. Do not include private information.
