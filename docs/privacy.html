Privacy policy

Floatdoro is designed to work without collecting personal data.

Data collection

Floatdoro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking technology, account system, or third-party SDK.

Data stored on your Mac

Timer state, tasks, preferences, completed-task history, and focus-session history are stored locally inside the app’s macOS sandbox. This information is not sent to the developer or any third party.

System services

When enabled, Floatdoro uses macOS notifications, sound, and launch-at-login services. These features are provided by macOS and do not cause Floatdoro to collect data.

Changes

If Floatdoro’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public support channel on the support page.
